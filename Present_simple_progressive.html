Simple Present vs Present Progressive

Instructions:
Complete the text with the correct form of the verb. Choose between Simple Present and Present Progressive. Pay attention to words such as usually, every day, always, now, today, at the moment, and the context.
My name is Emma, and I (live) with my parents and my younger brother in a small town. We (have) a comfortable house near the city center. My father (work) in a bank, and my mother (teach) English at a local school. My brother Daniel is 12 years old. He usually (play) soccer with his friends after school. He also (like) video games, but he doesn't play them every day.

Today is Saturday, and things are different. Right now, my father (cook) lunch in the kitchen. My mother (watch) TV in the living room, and Daniel (play) a new video game. I (study) English in my bedroom because I have an exam next week.

During the week, I normally (get up) at seven o'clock. I (eat) breakfast with my family, and then I (go) to school by bus. My classes (start) at eight thirty and usually (finish) at two o'clock.

This week, however, I (prepare) for an important English exam. I (read) more books than usual, and I (practice) English every evening.

I really (enjoy) studying languages, but I don't usually (study) on Saturday. Today, though, I (work) hard because I want to get a good grade.
