📚 Grammar Help

1. Subject Pronouns

Use subject pronouns as the subject of a sentence.

I, you, he, she, it, we, they

Example: She works at a school.

2. Object Pronouns

Use object pronouns after verbs or prepositions.

me, you, him, her, it, us, them

Example: I like him.

3. Possessive Adjectives

Possessive adjectives come before a noun.

my, your, his, her, its, our, their

Example: This is my book.

4. Possessive Pronouns

Possessive pronouns replace the noun. They are not followed by a noun.

mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs

Example: This book is mine.
